Travel information

  • National park boundaries, opening seasons, and trail information: Administracion de Parques Nacionales, Argentina (argentina.gob.ar/parquesnacionales).
  • Destination descriptions and seasonal advice: Secretaria de Turismo de la Nacion, Argentina (argentina.travel), and the provincial tourism boards of Neuquen, Rio Negro, Chubut, Santa Cruz, and Tierra del Fuego.
  • Whale and penguin season dates for the Chubut coast: Secretaria de Turismo de Chubut.
  • UNESCO World Heritage listings for Peninsula Valdes, Cueva de las Manos, and Los Alerces National Park: UNESCO World Heritage Centre (whc.unesco.org).
