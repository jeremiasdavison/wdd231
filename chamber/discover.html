Discover Meridian Falls

About the Area

Meridian Falls sits where Silver Creek drops into the Snake River canyon in southwestern Idaho, 45 minutes from Boise. The valley pairs an outdoor season that runs all twelve months with a walkable downtown of locally owned shops, and it is one of the fastest growing small cities in the state.

  • 14,860 residents
  • 36.4 median age
  • 3,120 ft elevation
  • 212 sunny days a year
  • 640 chamber member businesses
  • 18 min average commute
