Site Plan: Wild Patagonia

WDD 231 — Individual Project

1. Site Name

Wild Patagonia

The name was chosen because it is short, easy to remember, and immediately communicates the subject of the site: the untamed, natural beauty of the Argentine Patagonia region. It works well in English for an international audience planning a trip and pairs naturally with imagery of mountains, glaciers, and wildlife.

2. Site Purpose

Wild Patagonia exists to give travelers a simple, reliable guide to Argentine Patagonia. The site provides:

  • Overviews of the most visited destinations, including Bariloche, El Calafate, Ushuaia, and El Chalten.
  • Details on the main attractions in each destination, such as Perito Moreno Glacier, Fitz Roy, and Nahuel Huapi Lake.
  • Practical travel information: weather patterns, the best time of year to visit, and how to get to each destination.

3. Scenarios

Scenario questions that reflect what a target user would ask when visiting the site:

  1. "I'm planning a trip to see Perito Moreno Glacier — what is the best time of year to go, and how do I get to El Calafate?"
  2. "I want to hike near Fitz Roy — what trails are available around El Chalten, and what should I know before I go?"

4. Color Schema

Color Swatch Hex Where it is used
Glacier Blue #1B4B6B Header background, navigation, section headings
Sunset Orange #E8763C Accent color for buttons, links, and highlights
Andes Stone #4A4A48 Body text color
Snow White #F7F5F0 Page and card background

5. Color Schema Use in Document

This document itself uses the four colors defined above: Glacier Blue for the header and headings, Sunset Orange for links and accents, Andes Stone for body text, and Snow White for the page background, demonstrating how the palette will apply across the final site.

6. Typography Named and Used

Font Role Where and how it is applied
Playfair Display Heading font Used for all headings (h1, h2, h3) to give the site an elegant, travel-magazine feel. Applied to this document's headings.
Lato Body font Used for paragraphs, lists, and table text for clean readability on both mobile and desktop. Applied to this document's body copy.
